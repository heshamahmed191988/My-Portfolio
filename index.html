Selected work

Both are confidential client systems, so instead of screenshots each case shows the shape of the workflow I built.

CASE 01 Sharjah Government · UAE 2024 — present

SPEA Support Services Management Platform

An enterprise platform that digitalises financial support services for a government authority.

Problem

Financial support services ran on paper: budget requests, transfers and invoices moved by hand, approvals were hard to track, and there was no single view of spending or commitments.

What I built

  • Five financial modules — Annual Budget Preparation, Purchase Requests, Budget Transfers, Budget Additions and Invoice Management.
  • A configurable workflow engine: role-based access, delegation, dual approval and automated email, SMS and in-app notifications.
  • A KPI dashboard for department spending, pending requests, SLA compliance and financial commitments.
  • Security by design: AES-256, TLS 1.3, 2FA, full audit trail and rate limiting, compliant with UAE Data Protection Law No. 45/2021.

Stack

  • ASP.NET MVC
  • C#
  • SQL Server
  • RBAC
  • 2FA
  • AES-256
  • TLS 1.3

Outcome

Paper-based financial requests replaced by one auditable digital workflow, with live visibility for management.

CASE 02 Medina Municipality · KSA 2024 — present

Amanat Al Madinah Laboratory Management System

A laboratory and ERP system for construction-material testing and the finance behind it.

Problem

Asphalt, aggregate and soil test results needed structured capture, a controlled approval path before release, and reliable reporting — alongside core accounting operations.

What I built

  • Testing modules for asphalt, aggregate and soil with structured data entry and result tracking.
  • Lab-result approval pipelines with Excel and PDF reporting.
  • Contributions to MFA, AI-assisted analysis and rate limiting.
  • Core ERP modules: Journal, Invoices, Taxes and Stock Management.

Stack

  • .NET
  • C#
  • SQL Server
  • MFA
  • Excel / PDF
  • ERP

Outcome

Test results captured once, approved through a defined pipeline and issued as consistent reports — with finance in the same system.
